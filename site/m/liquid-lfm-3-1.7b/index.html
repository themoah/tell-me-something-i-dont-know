Honey never spoils

Most people assume that once you pour honey into a jar it will eventually turn sour, mold, or become “sour honey” after a few days. In reality, natural honey can last for thousands of years in a cool, dry place—like an ancient Egyptian tomb or a cave—if the right conditions are met.

Why?

  1. Very low water activity (≈17%). Honey isn’t 100 % liquid; it’s mostly sugar crystals dissolved in a tiny bit of water, so there aren’t enough available molecules for most microorganisms to grow.

Because of these properties, archaeologists have uncovered jars of honey that were still sealed and edible over 3,000 years ago, and even honey stored in tropical climates has resisted decay far beyond expectations.

So your next batch of raw honey can stay out overnight or even months without worrying about it turning into something unpleasant—just let it sit, and you’ll still have sweetness that lasts forever. 🌿🍯
