Your eyes have a completely separate immune system from the rest of your body.

This is known in medicine as ocular immune privilege.

Because swelling and inflammation in the eye can easily destroy delicate tissues and cause blindness, your body evolved to exclude the eye from your normal, aggressive immune system. Instead, the eye manages its own micro-environment to keep inflammation to an absolute minimum.

The crazy part? Your main immune system doesn't actually know your eyes exist.

If you suffer a severe eye injury that causes eye fluid to leak into your bloodstream, your main immune system can "discover" eye proteins for the first time. Because it has never seen them before, it misidentifies them as dangerous foreign invaders.
