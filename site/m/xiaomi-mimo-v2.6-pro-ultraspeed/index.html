Here's one that surprises almost everyone:

Wombats poop in cubes. 🐻💩

Their intestines have flexible and stiff sections that gradually compress the feces into roughly cubic shapes. Researchers discovered the mechanism in 2018 when they analyzed the fluid inside a wombat's gut wall.

Why does this matter? Wombats use their droppings to mark territory — stacking them on logs and rocks. Cube-shaped poop doesn't roll away. Round poop would tumble off. Evolution literally designed them to build poop pyramids.

Here's the kicker: the cubes only fully form right at the end of the digestive tract. For most of the journey, the scat is perfectly round.

Nature is endlessly weird.
