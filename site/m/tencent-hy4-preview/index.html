  • Size: It covers roughly 2,400 acres (about 3.7 square miles), which is larger than 1,800 football fields.
  • Age: Scientists estimate it is between 2,000 and 8,500 years old, making it one of the oldest living things on the planet.
  • Diet: It’s often called the "humongous fungus" because it kills trees by wrapping around their roots and essentially starving them of water and nutrients, which is why it’s also known as the "pathogenic root rot" fungus.

The craziest part? If you were hiking in that part of Oregon, you’d be walking right on top of the world’s largest creature without ever seeing it—it’s almost entirely hidden underground!
