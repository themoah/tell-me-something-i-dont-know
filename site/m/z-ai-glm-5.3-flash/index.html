Hard to guarantee, since I don't know what you know — but here's one most people haven't heard:

Woolly mammoths were still alive when the pyramids were built.

The Great Pyramid of Giza was finished around 2560 BCE. Meanwhile, a small population of mammoths survived on Wrangel Island, a remote spot in the Arctic Ocean off the coast of Siberia, until about 2000 BCE. So when the pyramid was already standing, mammoths were still walking the Earth — and they held on for another 500 years or so after that.

We file mammoths away with cave paintings and glaciers, but the timeline is much weirder than that: they overlapped with ancient Egypt, the Bronze Age, and the earliest writing systems.
