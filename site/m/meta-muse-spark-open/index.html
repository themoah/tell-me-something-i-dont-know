I don't know what you know, so I'll gamble on something you probably don't:

A photon of light hitting your face today was born 100,000 years ago.

Light takes 8 minutes to get from the Sun to Earth, but that's just the last leg of the trip. Photons are created by fusion in the Sun's core, and then spend 100,000 to 1 million years bouncing around randomly inside the Sun's dense radiative zone trying to escape. Once they finally break free of the surface, then they sprint to you in 8 minutes.

So sunlight is ancient light on a super fast delivery.

Here are two more on the house, in case you knew that one:

1. Trees have an internet. Forests are connected underground by a massive network of fungi called mycorrhizae - scientists call it the "Wood Wide Web." Trees use it to share nutrients and send chemical warning signals. Mother trees will even pump extra sugars to their sick offspring through it.

2. There is a biologically immortal animal. The jellyfish Turritopsis dohrnii never really dies of old age. When it's injured, sick, or just old, it can revert its cells back to childhood and turn back into a baby polyp. Then it starts its life over. It can do this indefinitely.

3. Shuffling cards creates something that has never existed before. There are 8.07 x 10^67 ways to shuffle a 52-card deck. That's 80,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000 ways.

Every time you shuffle well, you are almost certainly holding an arrangement of cards that has never existed in human history and will never exist again.

Want more? Give me a category - space, body, history, psychology, animals - and I'll go deeper down the rabbit hole.
