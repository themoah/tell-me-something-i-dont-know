Bet you didn't know this one:

You are blind for about 40 minutes every single day and your brain is lying to you about it.

Every time your eyes move - which they do 3-4 times per second - your brain literally shuts off your vision so you don't see a blurry smear. It's called saccadic masking. Your brain then edits the gap and fills it in with what it thinks you should have seen. You're hallucinating your stable, smooth reality.

You can prove it: Look in a mirror and flick your eyes from one eye to the other. You'll never see your own eyes move, but anyone watching you will.

4. There's a jellyfish that is biologically immortal. Turritopsis dohrnii can revert back to its baby polyp stage after becoming an adult. It can theoretically do this forever, escaping death by just starting its life over.

Since I don't know what you do know, I'll give you 5 things that surprise almost everyone:

1. Sharks are older than trees and Saturn's rings

Sharks have existed for ~400 million years. Trees only showed up ~350 million years ago. And Saturn's rings are relatively new - probably only 10-100 million years old. A shark would have looked at Earth and seen a barren landscape with no trees and a ring-less Saturn.

2. There are more ways to shuffle a deck of cards than atoms on Earth

A standard 52-card deck has 8.06 x 10^67 possible orders. That's an 8 with 67 zeros after it. There are only about 10^50 atoms on Earth. Every time you shuffle a deck well, you have almost certainly created an order of cards that has never existed before in human history.

3. Cleopatra lived closer to you than to the pyramids being built

The Great Pyramid of Giza was built around 2560 BCE. Cleopatra died in 30 BCE. That's a 2,530 year gap. You watching the Moon landing in 1969 is only 1,999 years after Cleopatra. She is closer to pizza delivery apps than to the pyramids.

4. There's a planet where it rains molten glass sideways

Planet HD 189733b is a deep cobalt blue planet 64 light-years away. Its wind speeds hit 5,400 mph and its rain is literally shards of silicate glass blowing horizontally. And it smells like rotten eggs because its atmosphere is full of hydrogen sulfide.

5. Your stomach would digest itself if it didn't rebuild itself every 3 days

Your stomach acid is strong enough to dissolve razor blades. The only reason it doesn't eat through your stomach wall is that your entire stomach lining replaces itself every 3-4 days. You literally have a new stomach multiple times a month.

Bonus one you can test right now: You are blind and don't know it.

You have a blind spot in each eye where the optic nerve connects - there are no light receptors there. Your brain just photoshops over it using the surrounding pattern.
